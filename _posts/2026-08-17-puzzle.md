I'm excited to announce _Puzzle_, my latest solo exhibition, and a conceptually-related-companion group show called _Keep It Together_, opening at [Wallkill River Center For the Arts](https://wallkill.art/events/category/gallery-events/), 232 Ward Street, Montgomery, NY on Saturday, August 22 from 5:00 to 7:00 pm. The shows run through September 27. Gallery hours are Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, 12:00 to 5:00 PM.

In addition to my solo show, I am pleased to have juried _Keep It Together_, which also features assemblage-based artwork that conveys a material and conceptual sense of solving and/or coping. The wonderful artists are: Toluwalope Ayorinde, Julia Barbour, Jeannine Bardo, Jenne Currie, Melanie Delgado, Tasha Depp, Karen E. Gersch, Lucy Handley, Jen Hicks, Chong Kang, Jeff & Wilson Keithline, Tracy Leavitt, Jignasha Leonardo, Jennifer McGregor, Eva Melas, Ann Morris, Suzanne Parker, Eileen Power, Amanda Russo Rubman, Anthony Scarzafava, Melissa Stern, Linda Stillman, Kimberly Truitt, Christina Varga, Ward Yoshimoto, Mimi Young, and Leslie Zelamsky.
